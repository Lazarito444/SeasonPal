Ingredients

4 items listed

2 cans Cannellini beans (rinsed)
1 bunch Lacinato kale (chopped)
4 cloves Garlic (minced)
4 cups Vegetable broth

Cooking Steps

Step 1 to 2
1 Aromatics & Base

Heat 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil in the Dutch oven over medium heat. Sauté minced garlic until fragrant (about 60 seconds) without browning.

2 Simmer Beans & Greens

Pour in cannellini beans, vegetable broth, and sliced lacinato kale. Bring to a gentle simmer for 20 minutes until kale softens and broth turns silky.
