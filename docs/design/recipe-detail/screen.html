Pan-Seared Duck Breast with Spiced Blackberry Reduction

Preparation Steps Step 1 of 4
1 Prep & Skin Scoring
5 mins

Pat the duck breasts dry with kitchen paper. Score the duck skin in a fine 1/4-inch diamond crosshatch pattern, taking care not to cut through into the red meat. Season generously with flaky sea salt.

2 Cold-Pan Sear & Render
10 mins

Place skin-side down in a cold cast iron skillet over medium-low heat. Let render untouched until deep golden and crisp.
